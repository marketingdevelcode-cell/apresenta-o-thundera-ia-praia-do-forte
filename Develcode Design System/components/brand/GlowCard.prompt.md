Dark container with the deck's pale halo lift — wrap capability lists, case studies and product tiles in it.

```jsx
<GlowCard glow="halo" ghostNumber={1}>
  <ArrowLabel>Executive Intelligence</ArrowLabel>
  <p>Visão em tempo real da performance da engenharia.</p>
</GlowCard>
```

Use `glow="blue"` only for the one card you want to be the focus of a slide. `tone="light"` switches to the white paper register.
