The brand's most recognisable element — a pill label led by a circled ↘ arrow; use it for eyebrows and capability names.

```jsx
<ArrowLabel tone="onDark" size="md">Product Discovery</ArrowLabel>
```

Tones: `onDark` (glowing blue outline over near-black), `onLight` (blue text, white fill, blue outline), `solid` (filled brand blue). `showArrow={false}` gives a plain pill for chips and result rows. `full` stretches the pill across its container — that is how the capability boxes on the vertical pages are built (pill pulled 22px left so the arrow circle overhangs the card edge).
